# MLB26: Rise to the Bigs

MLB26: Rise to the Bigs is a fully cinematic, choice-driven baseball career simulator concept. The prototype in this repository presents a playable vertical slice of the pre-draft chapter, where every decision changes draft stock, reputation, clubhouse trust, pressure, player archetype, and live story reactions.

## Experience Pillars

- **One-player career focus:** Play only your own at-bats, fielding chances, and baserunning moments while the story follows your player off the field.
- **Cinematic life choices:** Workouts, interviews, social media, family conversations, sponsorships, rivalries, friendships, and romances affect the season narrative.
- **Dynamic reputation:** Become a humble leader, flashy superstar, polarizing villain, or redemption-arc legend based on repeated choices.
- **Baseball legacy chase:** Grind from unknown prospect through the Minors to the Majors while chasing awards, records, championships, and a place in baseball history.

## Prototype

Open `index.html` in a browser to play the first interactive story beat. Choose how your prospect handles scout-day pressure and watch the dashboard, archetype, and reaction feed update immediately.

## Files

- `index.html` — page structure and playable career dashboard.
- `styles.css` — cinematic baseball presentation, responsive layout, and interactive choice styling.
- `game.js` — choice logic, career state changes, reputation labels, and live reactions.
